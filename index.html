Read them as phrases

The chords sit in musical order. 1–7 is G major going to A minor, through a D dominant, and home to Gmaj7. Play each row left to right and listen for it resolving.

Some grips skip the root

Chords like 10, 11 and 24 have no root note at all. The 3rd and 7th tell the ear what the chord is, and a bass player covers the root.

Two names, one grip

Chord 8 is Gmaj7 without its root, which is also a full Em9. The book promises to explain this later. Flip to intervals and you can see both readings.
